Help & Docs

Quick reference for getting the most out of VisionCrafter.

Prompt — describe the subject, setting, and mood. Short prompts get auto-expanded by the enhancer.

Negative Prompt — list what you don't want (defaults are pre-filled for common artifacts).

CFG Scale — how strictly the model follows your prompt; higher = more literal, lower = more creative freedom.

Steps — more denoising steps generally means more detail, at the cost of speed.

Seed — leave blank for a random result each time, or set a number to reproduce the same image.
